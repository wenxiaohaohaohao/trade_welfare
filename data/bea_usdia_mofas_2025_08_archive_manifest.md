## BEA Table Availability Note

BEA reports that selected annual data tables on the activities of U.S. multinational enterprises were discontinued or impacted on 2025-08-22, beginning with the 2023 preliminary data. These archived XLS files preserve the 2009-2023 country-industry downloadable tables used by this project.
